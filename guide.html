Patient guide

Prepare for China.

Records, costs, travel and daily life.

01

Medical records

Prepare diagnoses, imaging, pathology, treatment history, medication and allergies. Clear scans speed up specialist review.

02

Hospital confirmation

Suitability, physician availability and admission dates are confirmed by the hospital after record review.

03

Cost and payment

Planning ranges are preliminary. The hospital provides a case estimate; final charges follow actual care.

04

Visa and travel

Hospital documents, visa preparation, flights, airport transfer and accommodation are coordinated around the confirmed plan.

05

Language and daily life

Medical interpretation, dietary needs, companion arrangements and local transport can be included in the care plan.

06

Insurance and reimbursement

Confirm overseas coverage with your insurer before travel. We can organize hospital invoices and discharge documents.

07

Privacy and consent

Medical records are shared with hospitals only after patient authorization and for the agreed care purpose.

08

Urgent symptoms

For an emergency or severe new symptoms, contact local emergency services immediately. HUAYIAN CARE TRIP coordinates planned care.
