CASE OVERVIEW

Acute symptoms led to admission

A 61-year-old Japanese manager working in Wuhan had experienced intermittent chest pain for five years. A severe episode on 10 December led to hospital assessment the next day.

DIAGNOSISCoronary heart disease and acute myocardial infarction COMORBIDITIESDiabetes and hypertension

TREATMENT PLAN

Clinical plan

01

Stabilisation

Medication and management of blood pressure and blood glucose before intervention.

02

Specialist review

Cardiology review confirmed the need for coronary angiography and possible PCI.

03

PCI procedure

Coronary angiography and PCI were completed, with two stents placed.

04

Recovery

Post-procedure observation and recovery continued until discharge on 29 December.
