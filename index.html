FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Ask another question
How quickly can the process begin?

You can submit basic details now and choose a contact window. Specialist review and appointment timing begin only after the required records and hospital availability are confirmed.

Do I need to choose a hospital first?

No. Start with the medical goal and the relevant specialty. Hospital comparison becomes more useful after the clinical direction is clear.

How are hospitals matched?

The shortlist considers specialty capability, your records, city preference, timing and practical constraints. A directory rank is a research reference, not a guarantee of acceptance.

When do I upload medical records?

After the first consultation and consent step, through an agreed secure channel. The homepage form does not upload or transmit medical records.

Can you coordinate visas, flights and local travel?

Travel documents, arrival, interpreting and accommodation can be coordinated after the hospital plan is confirmed. Visa eligibility and entry rules depend on nationality and current policy.

Which languages and cultural needs can be supported?

Language interpreting, halal meals, prayer arrangements, family participation and privacy preferences can be recorded and coordinated. Availability must be confirmed for the selected hospital and city.

Is the estimate a hospital quote?

No. It is a planning range for medical care, stay and support. A formal hospital estimate follows record review and may change with the final treatment plan.

What if this is an emergency?

Contact local emergency services immediately. CareChina coordinates planned cross-border care and cannot replace emergency response.
