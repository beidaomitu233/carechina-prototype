Before you decide

Four questions worth confirming first.

Final answers depend on the hospital and your case.

Do I need to choose a hospital first?

No. Start with the condition; compare hospitals after the relevant specialty is clear.

When do I upload medical records?

After the first consultation and consent step, through a secure record channel.

Is the estimate a hospital quote?

No. It is a planning range. A hospital quote follows medical review.

What if this is an emergency?

Contact local emergency services immediately. This service is for planned care.
